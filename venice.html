Venice isn't one market

Venice contains dozens of distinct neighborhoods, golf communities, waterfront areas, and master-planned developments, so two homes with the same square footage can have very different market values. Home values vary significantly between Venice Island, East Venice, South Venice, Wellen Park, and North Venice — because of location, age of construction, waterfront access, golf communities, and newer master-planned developments. Here's a plain-language look at each, with recent recorded numbers where they help. Enter an address in any section for an instant estimate.

Venice Island

the historic island: downtown, the beaches, and Gulf-front living · ZIP 34285
Single-family median near $547,500 ($301/sq ft, the highest in Venice) · all home types including condos near $315,900

Includes Historic Venice, Golden Beach, and the gulf-front and island neighborhoods. Older, walkable, and close to the beach — island homes often sell at the highest price per square foot in Venice even when total prices run lower, because lot and location carry the value.

East Venice

the inland golf-and-established-neighborhood corridor · ZIP 34292
Single-family median near $486,250 ($235/sq ft) · all home types near $379,000

Includes the Jacaranda corridor, Plantation Golf & Country Club, Venice Acres, and Venice East. Established neighborhoods, larger lots, and golf-course communities inland from the island.

South Venice

the large, more affordable mainland area · ZIP 34293
Single-family median near $480,000 ($250/sq ft) · all home types near $415,000

Includes South Venice, Venice Gardens, and the Shamrock area. One of the larger and more affordable parts of Venice, with a wide mix of homes. Many South Venice homes are on septic systems; Sarasota County has an ongoing septic-to-sewer replacement effort and has identified South Venice as a future priority area — see the county program page and verify specifics for any address.

Wellen Park

the master-planned growth area (Venice mailing addresses) · ZIP 34293

Includes Gran Paradiso · Gran Paradiso homes for sale, Renaissance, Sunstone, Brightmore, Sarasota National, IslandWalk · IslandWalk homes for sale, and newer neighborhoods still being built. Primarily uses Venice mailing addresses, though much of Wellen Park sits within the City of North Port and some extends into unincorporated Sarasota County. New construction here commands different pricing than Venice's established areas. See the full Wellen Park communities guide →

North Venice

the northern edge toward the Nokomis line · ZIP 34275/34292

Includes Toscana Isles, Venetian Golf & River Club, Bellacina, and Milano. Often associated with Venice but geographically closer to Nokomis, these newer gated and golf communities straddle the Venice–Nokomis line.

Comparing areas of Venice

A plain-language look at what distinguishes each part of Venice. These are factual distinctions, not rankings — the right area depends on what you're looking for.

Is Wellen Park part of Venice?

Wellen Park primarily uses Venice mailing addresses, but much of it sits within the City of North Port, and some extends into unincorporated Sarasota County. It's commonly associated with Venice because of the mailing address.

What's the difference between North Venice and South Venice?

North Venice, near the Nokomis line, is known for newer master-planned and golf communities. South Venice is a larger, more affordable mainland area with a mix of older homes, many on septic systems. They sit on opposite ends of the Venice area.

Which part of Venice has the newest homes?

Wellen Park and North Venice have the most new construction, with master-planned communities still being built. Venice Island and South Venice are more established, with older housing stock.

Which area of Venice is most walkable?

Venice Island, with its historic downtown, shops, restaurants, and beach access within a compact area, is the most walkable part of Venice.

Which areas have the most master-planned communities?

Wellen Park and North Venice. Wellen Park includes communities like Gran Paradiso, IslandWalk, and Sarasota National; North Venice includes Toscana Isles, Venetian Golf & River Club, and others.

Venice home value questions

What is the average home price in Venice, FL?

Over the past year, single-family homes in Venice sold at a median recorded price of $490,000, about $250 per square foot, across 1,839 sales in Sarasota County public records. Across all home types the median was $400,000.

Which part of Venice is most expensive?

It depends how you measure. Venice Island (34285) often shows the highest price per square foot because of its location and older, smaller homes, while newer construction in Wellen Park and larger homes in East Venice can carry higher total prices. Values vary significantly by section.

Is Wellen Park in Venice or North Port?

Both, in a sense. Wellen Park primarily uses Venice mailing addresses, but much of it sits within the City of North Port, and some extends into unincorporated Sarasota County. That's why you'll see it associated with both.

How big is the average Venice home?

The average single-family home that sold in Venice this past year was about 2,016 square feet. Homes in the city were built between 1916 and 2025, one of the widest ranges in Sarasota County.
