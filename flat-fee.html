What Is Flat Fee MLS?

Flat Fee MLS Explained Simply

The MLS — Multiple Listing Service — is the database all licensed Realtors use to find homes for their buyers. When your home is on the MLS it appears automatically on Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, and hundreds of other real estate websites. The MLS is where buyers find homes.

Traditionally, getting your home on the MLS required hiring a listing agent at 2.5-3% of your sale price — on a $600,000 home that's $15,000-$18,000 — just for the listing side, before the buyer's agent commission.

Flat fee MLS changes this. You pay a one-time flat fee to get your home listed on the MLS. You keep full control of your sale. You save the traditional listing commission. Putnam Realty Group offers flat fee MLS listings starting at $499 — and every level includes pricing intelligence from recent local sales built from actual sales in your specific neighborhood.

Is Flat Fee Right for Me?

When Flat Fee MLS Makes Sense — and When It Doesn't

Flat Fee MLS Is a Great Fit If You:

  • Have sold a home before and are comfortable with the basic process — contracts, contingencies, timelines
  • Have a home in strong demand where multiple offers are likely and negotiation complexity is lower
  • Are flexible on timing and don't need to sell in a compressed window
  • Are comfortable communicating directly with buyer's agents and buyers
  • Are selling in a community you know well — like a neighbor buying your home in the same subdivision

You May Want Level 2 or Full Service If You:

  • Are selling for the first time and want professional guidance at every step
  • Have a complex situation — divorce, estate, distressed property, or unusual title issues
  • Need to sell quickly and want a professional pricing and marketing strategy from day one
  • Have a luxury or unique property where pricing precision and premium marketing matter significantly
  • Are relocating and can't be present for showings, inspections, and closing coordination
Frequently Asked Questions

Flat Fee MLS Questions Answered

Will buyer's agents show my home if it's a flat fee listing? +
Yes — as long as you offer a competitive buyer's agent commission in the MLS, buyer's agents will show your home. The listing side commission (what you pay your listing agent) is invisible to buyer's agents. What they see is the commission offered to them — typically 2-2.5% in our market. If you offer a fair buyer's agent commission, your home will receive the same showing activity as any traditional listing.
What is a Limited Service listing vs Full Service? +
A Limited Service listing means the listing broker's obligations are limited to placing the home on the MLS and providing basic documentation. The seller takes on more responsibility for communications, showings, and negotiations. A Full Service listing means the broker actively represents the seller through the entire transaction including negotiations, contract management, and closing coordination. Florida law requires that the service level be clearly disclosed in the listing agreement and the MLS — buyers and their agents know which type of listing they are dealing with.
What happens when I get an offer? +
With Level 1, offers come directly to you. You review them, negotiate directly with the buyer's agent, and sign contracts yourself. The AI assistant can walk you through the key terms of any offer, but the decisions are yours. With Level 2, Michael reviews the offer with you and explains every term — purchase price, contingencies, financing type, closing timeline, and any seller contributions — so you can make a confident, informed decision before responding.
Does flat fee MLS work in Sarasota County? +
Yes — flat fee MLS is fully legal and actively used in Sarasota County and throughout Florida. The Stellar MLS, which covers Sarasota, Manatee, Charlotte, and surrounding counties, accepts limited service listings. Your home appears on the same MLS as every other listed home, and buyers and buyer's agents have no way to distinguish a flat fee listing from a traditional listing in most search results.
How do I know what to price my home at? +
Every Putnam Realty Group service level includes access to our AI home valuation tool — calibrated from recent comparable sales in recent local sales data. This gives you a data-driven starting point for pricing based on what homes like yours have actually sold for, not national averages or automated estimates that don't know your community.
