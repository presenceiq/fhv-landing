Buying new from Neal, or buying a resale?

Grand Palm is one of the few communities in this area where a buyer still has both options, and that shapes what an existing home is worth. Neal Communities is marketing its final phase here, with new homes advertised from $400,990 and quick move-in inventory listed at $895,990, $984,990 and $999,990, scheduled to complete through the fourth quarter of 2026.

Set that against what existing homes actually closed at over the past year: a median of $539,000 for single-family resales and $417,000 for villas, with the full resale range running from $271,900 to $1,125,000.

Two things follow. A buyer comparing a new Neal home to a resale is usually comparing a base price against a finished home — the resale typically already has the landscaping, the window treatments, sometimes the pool and the lanai, and a lot that has had years to fill in. A seller listing today is competing against a builder who can offer incentives and a warranty, but who cannot offer a mature lot or immediate occupancy.

Grand Palm, in plain terms

Grand Palm is a gated, nature-focused master-planned community in Venice, developed by Neal Communities and set within the Wellen Park area. It spans about 1,000 acres, roughly a third of it preserved as conservation and open space, laced with miles of walking and biking trails, boardwalks, and a large central lake with its own island. Neal deliberately kept old-growth oaks, sabal palms, and palmetto — the “Old Florida” look. Homes have been built from about 2010 onward, and while much of the community is established, Neal still has new-construction villages selling. It is not age-restricted.

Why one Grand Palm home is worth far more than a similar one nearby

This is the heart of value in Grand Palm: location within the neighborhood often matters as much as the house itself. Two similar floor plans can sell for very different numbers depending on the lot. The biggest swing factors are preserve, lake, or greenbelt view; lot privacy (a preserve lot with no rear neighbors is a real premium here); pool; floor plan and model; which village the home sits in; outdoor living space; and kitchen and bath upgrades. Because Neal built the community around preserve and water rather than a golf course, view and lot location carry unusually large premiums. A national estimate can't see any of this; a local comparative market analysis prices it in.

Home types behave like separate micro-markets

Grand Palm offers paired villas, villas, cottages, single-family homes, and larger estate homes, roughly 1,500 to 2,900+ square feet. In practice these behave like separate micro-markets — a paired villa and an estate home draw different buyers and price differently. Several sub-villages within Grand Palm are separately gated and have their own pools, creating distinct micro-locations inside one community.

A nature community, not a golf community

Grand Palm's defining feature is its natural setting — roughly a third of the land is open space, with wildlife corridors, dog parks, an island park, and a central lake for kayaking and paddleboarding. That nature-first design, and its spread-out amenity areas, are what set it apart from golf-centric or single-clubhouse communities nearby, and it's why preserve and lake lots are so in-demand.

Resale vs. remaining new construction

Because Neal Communities is still building and selling certain Grand Palm villages, resales compete directly with the builder's new homes and incentives. That competition is a real pricing factor — a resale has to be priced sensibly against the builder's net price (more on new vs. resale). Grand Palm is gated, with some villages separately gated (how gated-community fees work).

Location

Grand Palm sits in south Venice, roughly half a mile from I-75, about 15 to 20 minutes from the Gulf beaches and historic downtown Venice, and minutes from the Wellen Park town center and CoolToday Park (Atlanta Braves spring training).

Grand Palm vs. other Venice and Wellen Park communities

Buyers comparing Grand Palm are usually also weighing nearby communities, and the differences are clear. Where Sarasota National is built around bundled golf, Gran Paradiso around a Lennar gated-resort lifestyle, and Talon Preserve around new construction and the Legacy Trail, Grand Palm is the nature-and-preserve community — trails, conservation, and micro-locations rather than a course or a single town center. If you're also weighing IslandWalk, that's a lagoon-and-town-center feel; Grand Palm leans natural and spread-out.
