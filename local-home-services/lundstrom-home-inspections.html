What buyers and sellers ask before an inspection

How fast will I get the report?

Same day as the inspection in most cases. Reports are digital, include photographs, and come with repair addendums and a PDF version you can hand straight to your agent.

I am selling. Is it worth inspecting before I list?

It puts you ahead of the buyer's inspector. Knowing what will come up lets you price it in or fix it on your own timeline rather than renegotiating under pressure a week before closing.

What are 4-point and wind mitigation reports, and do I need them?

They are the Florida-specific reports insurers ask for. A 4-point covers roof, electrical, plumbing and HVAC. Wind mitigation documents features that can lower your premium. Lundstrom handles both, and bundles them for buyers who need several at once.

What makes Scott different from other inspectors?

He spent years in the building trades before inspecting, so he walks a property having built and repaired the systems he is looking at. Every inspection includes a free home maintenance book, free thermal imaging, and a walk-through afterwards explaining what was found.

Which areas does he cover?

North Port, Venice, Nokomis, Osprey, Sarasota, Englewood and Port Charlotte, and across Southwest Florida.
