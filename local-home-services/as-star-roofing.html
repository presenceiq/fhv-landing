What homeowners ask before a roof job

My inspection came back with roof issues before closing. Can that be fixed in time?

Often yes. A's Star Roofing handles repairs as well as full replacements and responds quickly on leaks and emergencies, which is usually what a closing timeline needs.

Does a roof's age affect what my home is worth?

In Florida it affects more than value. Insurers look closely at roof age and condition, and an older roof can make a policy harder to place, which in turn narrows the pool of buyers who can close.

What roof types do they work on?

Shingle, tile, metal, TPO and flat roofing systems, on both homes and commercial buildings, from a single repair through to full replacement.

Which areas do they cover?

Venice, Nokomis, Englewood and Sarasota, extending to Port Charlotte, Arcadia, Fort Myers and across Southwest Florida.

Are they licensed?

The owner states the business is licensed and insured. Confirm any Florida contractor license free at myfloridalicense.com before hiring.
