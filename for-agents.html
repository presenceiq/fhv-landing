Frequently Asked Questions

Questions from Agents

How is this different from a generic home value widget on my website? +
Generic home value widgets pull from national AVM databases calibrated at the zip code or county level. Florida Home Value AI is built exclusively from your territory's recent comparable sales — floor plan by floor plan, using Sarasota County public records for that specific community. A subdivision-level estimate reflects your market far more closely than a broad zip-code or county-wide average.
Do I need to know how to code or build websites? +
No. You provide your territory details and your contact information. Michael and the Florida Home Value AI platform build, deploy and maintain everything, including lead capture and the notification emails. Going live is a single file upload to Cloudflare Pages, and Michael walks you through it the first time.
Where does the valuation data come from? +
Every territory tool is calibrated exclusively from Sarasota County public records — using the recorded sale price of every qualified sale in that community. No MLS data is used, derived, or displayed in the valuation, which keeps the tool compliant for public, consumer-facing use. Michael handles the county data sourcing and calibration for you.
What happens when market conditions change? +
The valuation engine is calibrated from your territory's recent sales data at the time of build. As new sales close, Michael recalibrates the engine from updated county records — typically quarterly or when significant market shifts occur. The monthly performance report tracks any drift between estimates and actual sales, flagging when recalibration is needed.
Can I use this for multiple subdivisions? +
Yes — each subdivision is a separate territory license. Agents serving multiple gated communities or neighborhoods can license multiple territories. Each gets its own branded tool, its own lead capture, and its own market report. The landing page at floridahomevalueai.com lists all your live territories so buyers and sellers can find their specific community.
Is my territory truly exclusive? +
Yes. Florida Home Value AI licenses one agent per subdivision. Once you license a territory, no other agent can purchase that same community. Your exclusivity is maintained for as long as you hold an active territory license.
Can I white-label this for my brokerage? +
White-label brokerage licensing is available for teams and brokerages wanting to deploy multiple territory tools under a unified brand. Contact Michael directly to discuss brokerage-level arrangements, seat-based pricing, and co-branding options.
