Neighborhood research · Palmer Ranch

Palmer Ranch is not one place. It is 24 of them.

Measured against the Sarasota County certified tax roll, the 24 Palmer Ranch subdivisions with more than 120 parcels have median assessed values ranging from $79,444 to $588,977. Owner occupancy runs from 16 percent to 85 percent. Arbor Lakes and Serenade are a mile apart and $375,000 apart.

The full guide covers every subdivision with its parcel count, median assessed value, owner-occupied share and pool share, plus the Legacy Trail, the schools, and which beaches are actually close.

Why It's Different
Real Data.
Local Intelligence.
Built on recorded sale prices from the county records, grouped by neighborhood, not by zip code.
📍
Subdivision-level data
Calibrated from actual sales in your specific neighborhood, not broad zip-code averages that lump your street with miles of other homes.
🏗️
Builder sales kept out
Every comparable is a sale between owners. Builder closings are left out, because a builder's price isn't a fair comparison for a home that's already been lived in.
🎯
You know your home best
The county record can't see an outdoor kitchen, a new roof or impact windows. In the community tools you tell it, and the estimate moves.
✅
Honest about limits
Every result says where its numbers come from and what records can't see, like condition, upgrades and view. It's a starting point, not an appraisal.
