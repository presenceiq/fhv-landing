Neighborhood research · Palmer Ranch

Palmer Ranch is not one place. It is 24 of them.

Measured against the Sarasota County certified tax roll, the 24 Palmer Ranch subdivisions with more than 120 parcels have median assessed values ranging from $79,444 to $588,977. Owner occupancy runs from 16 percent to 85 percent. Arbor Lakes and Serenade are a mile apart and $375,000 apart.

The full guide covers every subdivision with its parcel count, median assessed value, owner-occupied share and pool share, plus the Legacy Trail, the schools, and which beaches are actually close.
