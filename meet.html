Common questions

Should I meet a real estate agent before choosing one?
Yes - before you commit to anyone, sit down with them first, with no pressure and no obligation. You are about to hand someone the biggest sale of your life, and a brand name cannot tell you whether you will actually trust the person behind it.
Do I have to commit to anything to meet with you?
No - meet with me the same way you would meet any agent you are considering: just to see if we are a good fit. No sales pitch, no obligation, no pressure to list. Meet a few agents and pick the one you are most comfortable with.
What should I look for when choosing a listing agent?
Someone you genuinely trust and communicate well with, because the brand behind them matters far less than the person. Look for an agent who listens to your goals first, explains your options honestly, is local and reachable, and makes you feel comfortable.
