Is Wellen Park in Venice or North Port?

Wellen Park is a master-planned community in southern Sarasota County. While it uses Venice mailing addresses, much of Wellen Park is within the City of North Port, with some areas in unincorporated Sarasota County. Individual communities may fall under different jurisdictions, even though they share the Wellen Park identity. That's why you'll see it associated with both Venice and North Port — both are correct, depending on the specific community.

Wellen Park questions

Is Wellen Park in Venice or North Port?

Wellen Park uses Venice mailing addresses, but much of it is within the City of North Port, with some areas in unincorporated Sarasota County. Individual communities may fall under different jurisdictions while sharing the Wellen Park identity.

What communities are in Wellen Park?

Established communities include Gran Paradiso, IslandWalk, Grand Palm, Sarasota National, Renaissance, Sunstone, and Brightmore. Newer and current communities include Lakespur, Solstice, Everly, Oasis, Avelina, and Oakbend, with more still being built.

Where is Downtown Wellen?

Downtown Wellen is the walkable town center at the heart of Wellen Park, with shops, restaurants, a lake, and community events. It's the central gathering place for the surrounding communities.

Is Wellen Park still building?

Yes. Wellen Park is one of the fastest-growing master-planned communities in Southwest Florida, with several newer communities like Lakespur, Solstice, Oasis, Avelina, and Oakbend still under construction as of 2026.

What are the newest communities in Wellen Park?

Among the newest are Lakespur, Solstice, Everly, Oasis, Avelina, and Oakbend. Because they're new, they have limited resale history so far, most sales are builder closings.

Is there a 55+ community in Wellen Park?

Yes. Brightmore, by Mattamy Homes, is Wellen Park's first officially designated 55+ active adult community. The other communities are not age-restricted and are open to buyers of any age.
