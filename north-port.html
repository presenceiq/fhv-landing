North Port isn't one market

North Port includes much of Wellen Park, one of Southwest Florida's fastest-growing master-planned communities, while other neighborhoods range from established canal-front homes to newer construction along the I-75 corridor. Because North Port covers a large geographic area, home values can vary significantly depending on the neighborhood, waterfront location, age of the home, and proximity to shopping, parks, and major roadways.

Waterfront canal homes, golf-course communities, and properties near Warm Mineral Springs each draw their own buyer interest. Proximity to I-75, U.S. 41, and the CoolToday Park spring-training complex also shapes demand across different parts of the city. About 25% of North Port homes have a pool.

What's coming to North Port

North Port is one of the fastest-growing cities in the nation, and major investment is following the growth, the kind of thing that tends to support long-term demand:

  • Sarasota Memorial Hospital – North Port. A $507 million, nine-story, full-service hospital on a 32-acre site at North Sumter Boulevard near I-75, the city's first acute-care hospital. Scheduled to open in 2028, opening with 100 beds and built to expand to 208 and beyond.
  • AdventHealth has purchased a 17.4-acre parcel off U.S. 41 for an additional healthcare facility.
  • City officials have described North Port as becoming a growing medical and services hub for south Sarasota County, with several projects in early planning stages.

Public water & sewer vs. well & septic

North Port runs its own city water and sewer utility, but not every home is connected. Many homes - especially older ones - are on private well and septic, while public water and sewer is a feature many buyers prefer and will pay attention to. Service availability varies by address as the city expands its systems.

You can check whether public water and sewer are available at a specific address using the City of North Port water lookup, or see the North Port Utilities Department for details.
