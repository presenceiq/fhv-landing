Palmero vs Talon Preserve

Two of Nokomis's newest gated communities, compared on real recorded Sarasota County sales, not national averages.

Over the past year, Talon Preserve homes sold at a higher median ($639,400) and a notably higher price per square foot ($323) than Palmero ($556,850, $196/sq ft). But Palmero's homes are larger at the median (2,632 vs 1,909 sq ft), so buyers are weighing price per foot against total space. Both are new-construction communities still being built out.

What the numbers say

The clearest difference is price per square foot: Talon Preserve commands about $323 versus Palmero's $196. But total price tells a subtler story, because Palmero's homes run larger at the median, a bigger Palmero home can land near a smaller Talon home on total price while costing much less per foot. Pools are also more common in Talon (41% vs 11%), which lifts its numbers, since pool homes sell at a premium.

Both communities are new and still selling, so their recorded sales include a mix of resales and builder closings, and the sample is smaller than an established neighborhood. Common questions

Is Palmero or Talon Preserve more expensive?

Talon Preserve had a higher median sale price ($639,400) and a higher price per square foot ($323) than Palmero ($556,850, $196/sq ft) over the past year in Sarasota County records. However, Palmero's homes are larger at the median, so total price and price per foot point different directions.

Which community has larger homes?

Palmero, at the median. Palmero's median home was about 2,632 square feet versus roughly 1,909 in Talon Preserve, based on recorded sales.

Are these new-construction communities?

Yes. Both were built primarily from 2020 onward and are still being built out, so recorded sales include builder closings alongside resales, and sample sizes are smaller than established neighborhoods.

Where are Palmero and Talon Preserve located?

Both are gated communities in Nokomis, FL (Sarasota County), near the 34275 area, a short drive from Venice, the Legacy Trail, and Gulf beaches.
