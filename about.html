Meet Michael Putnam

A local real estate Sales Associate who answers the phone, knows Sarasota County street by street, and gives you a straight answer.

I'm Michael Putnam, a licensed Florida real estate Sales Associate with Putnam Realty Group, serving Venice, Nokomis, Osprey, North Port, Wellen Park, and the surrounding Sarasota County communities.

I've spent about 25 years in real estate and have lived in Sarasota County for roughly 16 of them. Before Florida, I grew up in Weymouth, Massachusetts, just south of Boston. Over the years I've owned and managed rental and vacation properties, and worked with single-family homes, condos, beach homes, and manufactured homes. That hands-on experience, including years spent studying property values closely, is why I can look at a home and talk honestly about what actually drives its price - not just quote you a number.

That's also why I built Florida Home Value AI: to give homeowners a real, data-backed starting point using Sarasota County public records, then a straight conversation if they want one. No call center, no pressure. Just me.
