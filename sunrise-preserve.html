Sunrise Preserve, in plain terms

Sunrise Preserve is a smaller gated community in Palmer Ranch (Sarasota), built by Mattamy Homes — about 300+ single-family homes and 50+ villas, roughly 400 homes in all, built between 2018 and 2024. It's maintenance-free (the HOA includes lawn care and common-area upkeep), has resort amenities without a golf course, and notably has no CDD assessment. It is not age-restricted.

Why one Sunrise Preserve home is worth more than another

Value here turns on a handful of specific things. The biggest swing factors: villa vs. single-family (these behave like separate micro-markets); water view vs. interior lot; preserve view; pool vs. non-pool; outdoor-living upgrades; and lot privacy — relatively few lots here have a fully private rear yard, so a private or preserve-backed lot carries a real premium. Because the community was built in a tight window (2018–2024), view, upgrades, and floor plan often matter more than a home's age. A national estimate can't see any of this; a local comparative market analysis prices it in.

What makes Sunrise Preserve different

Its identity is maintenance-free Palmer Ranch living with Mattamy architecture and a smaller-community feel. Mediterranean-style Mattamy homes; a lock-and-leave, lawn-care-included lifestyle; a footprint of roughly 400 homes rather than the scale of the large master-planned communities nearby; resort amenities (heated pool and spa, clubhouse, staffed fitness center, tennis, pickleball, bocce) with no bundled golf cost; and no CDD. That mix sits in an interesting middle ground — newer construction without the scale of a massive master-planned community.

Home types behave like separate micro-markets

Sunrise Preserve pairs single-family homes (roughly 2,000–2,700 square feet) with villas (roughly 1,500–1,700 square feet). They draw different buyers and price differently, so a villa sale isn't a reliable comp for a single-family home or vice versa.

Location

Sunrise Preserve sits in Palmer Ranch, about 1.7 miles from the Legacy Trail access at McIntosh Road and Palmer Ranch Parkway, and roughly 11 miles from downtown Sarasota, with its resort amenity center on site.
