Sunstone, in plain terms

Sunstone is a gated community in the Playmore District of Wellen Park (Venice), built by Mattamy Homes — single-family homes (roughly 1,500 to 2,969 square feet, in coastal, craftsman, West Indies, and modern styles) and attached villas (roughly 1,500 to 1,765 square feet). Unlike its more built-out neighbors, Sunstone is still actively building, with Mattamy models open daily and both quick move-in and to-be-built homes. It is not age-restricted.

What makes Sunstone different: two amenity campuses, no golf

Most communities have one amenity center. Sunstone has two — its original resort-style campus (beach-entry pool with lap lanes, spa, clubhouse, fitness center, pickleball, dog park, firepit, and a full-time activities director) plus a second amenity center in the newer Sunstone Lakeside enclave. And it delivers that amenity depth with no golf course or bundled golf cost. That combination — newer Mattamy homes, a deep amenity package, and no golf membership — is why Sunstone is often positioned as a strong overall value in Wellen Park.

Why one Sunstone home is worth more than another

Value here turns on specifics. The biggest swing factors: villa vs. single-family (separate micro-markets); preserve lot vs. interior lot; water view; pool vs. non-pool; proximity to the amenity centers; which section a home is in (original Sunstone vs. Sunstone Lakeside); outdoor-living upgrades; and premium lot orientation. Home types behave like separate micro-markets

Sunstone pairs attached villas (about 1,500–1,765 sq ft, lower-maintenance) with single-family homes up to roughly 2,969 sq ft in one- and two-story plans. They draw different buyers and price differently, so a villa sale isn't a reliable comp for a single-family home.

Resale vs. still-active new construction

This matters more in Sunstone than in most nearby communities: because Mattamy is still actively selling and incentivizing new homes here, a resale competes directly with the builder's new inventory and promotions. Pricing a resale against the builder's net price and current incentives is critical (more on new vs. resale). Sunstone is gated, with an HOA plus a CDD assessment on the tax bill, standard for Wellen Park (verify current amounts; how gated-community fees work).

Location

Sunstone sits in Wellen Park's Playmore District, close to Downtown Wellen's lakefront shopping, dining, and events, and CoolToday Park (Atlanta Braves spring training), with the Gulf beaches about 15 minutes away and easy access to US-41.

Sunstone vs. other Wellen Park communities

Buyers weighing Sunstone are usually also comparing nearby communities. Fellow Mattamy community Renaissance is more built-out with one amenity center; Grand Palm leans nature-and-preserve; and Sarasota National is built around bundled golf. Against those, Sunstone stands out as the newer, still-building option with two amenity campuses and no golf cost. If you're also weighing IslandWalk or Talon Preserve, Sunstone's active-builder pricing is a key difference.

Sunstone home value questions

What's my Sunstone home worth?

It depends heavily on villa vs. single-family, view, pool, which section (original Sunstone vs. Sunstone Lakeside), proximity to the amenity centers, and upgrades. Use the estimated value range above for a ballpark, then a local comparative market analysis for a real, address-specific number.

Does villa vs. single-family affect my home's value?

Yes. Attached villas (about 1,500–1,765 sq ft) and single-family homes (up to about 2,969 sq ft) draw different buyers and behave like separate micro-markets.

Who builds Sunstone?

Mattamy Homes. It's a gated community in the Playmore District of Wellen Park, Venice, still actively building new homes.

Is Sunstone a 55+ community?

No. Sunstone is not age-restricted and all ages are welcome. Wellen Park's dedicated 55+ community is Brightmore, not Sunstone.

Does Sunstone have CDD fees?

Yes. Like most Wellen Park communities, Sunstone has a CDD assessment on the annual tax bill in addition to HOA dues. Verify current figures for a specific home.
